Privacy Policy — Truck Hop

Developer: Qfxlab · Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Truck Hop ("the game"), developed by Qfxlab, handles information when you play it on Android or in a web browser.

Information we collect

The Android app and the game's normal play do not collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no account system of its own, and no form that asks for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal data.

The only exception is the optional Leaderboard in the CrazyGames web version of the game, described below. The Leaderboard does not exist in the Android app.

Leaderboard (CrazyGames web version only)

If you are logged in to your CrazyGames account and you finish a run, the game sends the following to our server (hosted on Cloudflare):

  • a signed token issued by CrazyGames that proves who you are on CrazyGames; our server verifies it and uses it to read your CrazyGames username and user ID;
  • the distance of your run, the hero you were using, and the time the run started and ended (used to detect impossible scores).

We store only a one-way hash (with a secret salt) of your CrazyGames user ID, your CrazyGames username, your best distance (all time and this week) and the hero. Your username, best distance and hero are shown publicly on the leaderboard to other players. Your IP address is used only to limit abuse; we keep just a salted hash of it for a few minutes and then delete it.

We do not collect your email address, real name, location, friends list or any other data through the leaderboard. We do not sell data or share it with anyone, except our infrastructure provider Cloudflare, which processes it on our behalf (this may happen outside your country). If you are not logged in to CrazyGames, nothing is sent to our server and you do not appear on the leaderboard.

Deleting your data: your leaderboard entry stays until you ask us to remove it. Email qfxlab@gmail.com with your CrazyGames username and we will delete it. Your CrazyGames account itself is managed by CrazyGames under its own privacy policy.

Game progress

Your game progress (gems, upgrades, unlocked or rented heroes, missions and settings) is saved only on your device (or, on CrazyGames, in the CrazyGames save system of your account). It is never sent to our own server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this progress.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The Android version of the game contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

When the game is played on a third-party web gaming platform, that platform may show its own ads and process data under its own privacy policy. Please refer to the policy of the platform you play on.

Permissions

The Android app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. Apart from the optional CrazyGames Leaderboard (which requires a CrazyGames account), we do not collect personal information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change (for example, if ads or analytics are added in a future version), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at qfxlab@gmail.com.
