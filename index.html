Privacy Policy — Truck Hop

Developer: Qfxlab · Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Truck Hop ("the game"), developed by Qfxlab, handles information when you play it on Android or in a web browser.

Information we collect

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account system, no sign-in, and no form that asks for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal data.

Game progress

Your game progress (gems, upgrades, unlocked or rented heroes, missions and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this progress.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The Android version of the game contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

When the game is played on a third-party web gaming platform, that platform may show its own ads and process data under its own privacy policy. Please refer to the policy of the platform you play on.

Permissions

The Android app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change (for example, if ads or analytics are added in a future version), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at qfxlab@gmail.com.
